GREENFIELD AMMONIA PLANT - FEASIBILITY STUDY (Rev B)

1. Project overview
The Greenfield plant is a new ammonia production facility located at the Port Alder industrial estate. The study assesses technical and commercial viability for a 20-year operating life.

2. Capacity and schedule
The plant is designed for an annual production capacity of 30,000 tonnes of ammonia. Mechanical completion and commissioning are planned for Q3 2026, with commercial operations starting immediately afterwards.

3. Capital cost
Total project capital expenditure (capex) is estimated at USD 48 million, including owner's costs and a 10% contingency.

4. Permitting and site
All environmental permits have been secured and the site has been cleared for construction. The site benefits from existing port access and a connection to the regional grid.

5. Market
Regional ammonia demand is forecast to grow at 3% per year. The plant would supply roughly 6% of regional demand at full capacity.
