ENGINEERING REVIEW - GREENFIELD AMMONIA PLANT

Scope
This independent review assesses whether the design basis is achievable and the delivery schedule is credible.

Capacity
The plant can reach 30,000 tonnes per year only if plant availability is at least 92% and feedstock purity stays above 99.5%. At the more typical first-years availability of 85%, expected output is about 27,500 tonnes per year.

Schedule
Based on the reactor lead time, mechanical completion is not expected before Q1 2027. Commissioning would follow in the second quarter of 2027.

Site conditions
Ground conditions at Port Alder are favourable and no major foundation risks were identified. Winter temperatures do not materially affect operations.
